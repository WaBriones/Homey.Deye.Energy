Your Deye hybrid inverter as a complete home energy meter in Homey.

The inverter appears as the home's grid meter, with grid power, imported and exported energy, today's grid cost, and the energy and cost for your billing cycle. The battery and solar panels are added as their own devices, so Homey Energy shows solar production, battery charge and discharge, home consumption and cost.

Set your electricity rate and the date your meter was last read in the inverter's settings.

The app uses the DeyeCloud API and your DeyeCloud login. Based on Deye Solar Systems by Andras Heszegi.
